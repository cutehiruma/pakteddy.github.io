The Clever Mouse Deer

Once upon a time, there lived a clever mouse deer named Kancil in a big forest in Indonesia. One sunny morning, Kancil was walking near the river looking for food.

Suddenly, he met a big crocodile named Buaya. The crocodile was very hungry and wanted to eat Kancil. "Ha ha! Today I will eat you for breakfast!" said the crocodile.

But Kancil was not afraid. He had a clever plan to escape from the dangerous crocodile.

What happened next?

Kancil thought quickly. "Wait!" he said to the crocodile. "Before you eat me, can you help me count how many crocodiles live in this river?"

The crocodile was curious. "Why do you want to know?" he asked.

"The King wants to give food to all the crocodiles," Kancil lied cleverly. "Please call all your friends and line up across the river so I can count you."

The Problem

In every story, there is always a problem or conflict. This is called the complication.

In our story, Kancil faced a dangerous situation. Let's find out what the problem was!

The Ending

The greedy crocodile believed Kancil's trick. He called all his friends and they lined up across the river.

"One... two... three..." Kancil counted while jumping on each crocodile's back. When he reached the other side, he laughed loudly.

"Ha ha! Thank you for the free bridge, silly crocodiles!" Kancil ran away quickly into the forest. The crocodiles were very angry, but it was too late. Kancil was safe!
